Measure your test print. If everything is 3 mm too high, enter 3 in “Move down”. Use a minus number to move the other way. Saved on this computer.

When printing: Paper size A4 · Scale 100% / “Actual size” (not “Fit to page”) · Margins Default · turn on “Background graphics” · turn off “Headers and footers”. Print from Google Chrome: Safari ignores these page settings and shifts the labels.
