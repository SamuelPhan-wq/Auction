Reset the auction

Use this after a test run, or to start over. It clears all bids and puts the auction back to not started (you'll click Start bidding again). The old bids are not deleted: they are kept in a tab called “Bids archive …” in your Google Sheet. Lot order, names, starting bids and the closing time are kept.
